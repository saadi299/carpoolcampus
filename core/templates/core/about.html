About CarpoolCampus

A simple, privacy-first carpool matching platform built specifically for students, staff, and faculty across Canadian post-secondary institutions.

Who CarpoolCampus is for

CarpoolCampus is designed exclusively for members of verified Canadian colleges and universities. When you register with your official institutional email (such as @uwaterloo.ca or @ubc.ca), you are automatically grouped into your school’s private network. You will only ever see and match with commuters from your own campus community.

How matching works

Finding a compatible carpool partner should not require broadcasting your personal life. Our matching algorithm connects you based on two simple factors:

  • General proximity: We group commuters based on their shared neighbourhood or Forward Sortation Area rather than exact street addresses.
  • Class schedule overlap: We compare departure and return times for days you travel to campus, highlighting matches with compatible schedules.

Once you review recommended matches, you can initiate a carpool request and decide together on exact pick-up spots, schedules, and shared commuting costs.

Privacy summary & data protection

Your privacy and safety are central to our design. Here is exactly what data is stored and what is visible to others:

Strict postal code truncation

Only the first 3 characters of your postal code (the Forward Sortation Area, such as K1P or V6T) are ever shown to other users. Your full street address or exact location is never requested, stored, or revealed.

Organization isolation

Your profile, schedule, and match suggestions are strictly isolated within your organization. Members from other institutions cannot view your profile or see your commute details.

No commercial tracking or data selling

CarpoolCampus is open-source. We do not sell your personal data, run advertising trackers, or share your commute habits with third parties.
