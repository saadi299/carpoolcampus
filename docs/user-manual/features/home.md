# Home page

## What you see

The home page at http://127.0.0.1:8000 introduces CarpoolCampus and shows three
steps:

1. **Join with your school email** — your email decides which organization you
   belong to.
2. **Add your timetable and postal code** — only the first part of your postal
   code is used to find people nearby.
3. **Get matched and share the ride** — see people near you with similar
   schedules.

## Try it

1. Start the app (see [Getting started](../getting-started.md)).
2. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000.
3. Check that you see the heading **"Share the ride to campus"** and the three
   numbered steps.
4. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin and log in as `admin` /
   `carpool-admin-demo` to confirm the admin panel works.
